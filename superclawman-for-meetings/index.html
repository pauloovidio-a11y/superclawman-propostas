What it does

Six capabilities, all working today.

📅

Auto-join from your calendar

Reads your calendar and joins scheduled Meet, Zoom, and Teams calls on its own — no link to paste, no bot to launch. Recurring meetings handled; automated/system events skipped.

🎙️

Live assist by voice or chat

Address it out loud mid-call and it raises its hand and answers the room; or type in the chat for a quiet reply. It can search the web live and pull from prior meetings.

🌎

Truly multilingual

Its own speech recognition handles accents and Portuguese where generic captions collapse. Heard “hey Superclawman” clean while the platform captions rendered “super climbing.”

🧠

Per-account memory

Every call is filed by account into a searchable journal. It walks into the next meeting already briefed on the history — decisions, objections, commitments.

✉️

Recap writes itself

Within minutes of the call ending: summary, decisions, action items with owners, and a recap email drafted to the attendees it knows from the invite. You approve and send.

📄

Call → proposal

“Prepare the proposal from that meeting” → a draft grounded in what was actually said, ready to polish into a branded document. Days-to-minutes on the follow-up.

How it works

A clean separation: a transport pipe gets it into the room; our own models do all the thinking.

Transport
Recall.ai is the pipe only — joins the call, streams audio in/out, posts chat. It never reasons. Reaches us over a stable Cloudflare named tunnel (zero dropped sockets).
Live brain
Our own speech-to-text (multilingual) + a fast reflex model with live web search → answers in seconds → our text-to-speech speaks back to the room. Wake-word only; self-muting so it never talks over itself.
Deep brain
After the call, a frontier model writes the brief — summary, decisions, action items, and whether it’s a sales conversation worth a proposal.
Memory
A canonical record per meeting + a per-account journal in shared storage you own, synced across every device and assistant. Greppable, queryable, durable.
Control plane
From a terminal or a Telegram channel: list calls, ask a grounded question, send-brief to attendees, proposal from a call. The post-call work lives where the document tools live.
Always-on
Five self-healing background services run the join, the live loop, the auto-finalize, the calendar watch, and the stable endpoint — restart on failure, survive reboot.
