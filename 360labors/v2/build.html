① What we need from you

To map your flow precisely and connect to your world. Most of this is "show us what you already do."

Sample data (the big one)

  • 3–5 clients' actual hours files as they really arrive — the Excel sheets, the emailed ones, photos of texted/WhatsApp hours. The messier the better.
  • One recent pay run end to end: the hours that went in, and the checks/ACH that came out.
  • A sample check (voided is fine) so we mirror the memo exactly — hours, range, tips, deductions.

QuickBooks & accounting

  • Your QuickBooks Desktop version/year, and whether your bookkeeper is open to us connecting to it (we never change how you use QB).
  • The name of the alternative system your bookkeeper mentioned ("starts with P") — so we weigh it before committing to a path.

Money rails

  • How workers get paid today by share: ACH/direct deposit vs PayPal / Venmo / CashApp (and which business accounts you hold).
  • Your Regions Bank online setup — whether ACH origination / bill-pay is already enabled.

Access & environment

  • Microsoft 365 / Outlook admin OK to let an agent read the mailbox where hours arrive (read-scoped).
  • Your internet speed test result (the home/office line), so we size the setup and decide on Starlink backup.
  • Confirmation the office machine stays on (you said it does) — that's where the platform lives.

That's it. No new software for you to learn, no change to how clients send hours or how your accountant works.

③ The week, by example

A real payroll week — say 200 workers across 35 clients — flowing through the system.

1

Hours arrive — however they come

Through the week, clients send hours their own way. The agent watches the Outlook mailbox + WhatsApp and picks them up automatically.

e.g. Client A: Excel attached to email · Client B: a photo of a handwritten ticket via text · Client C: hours typed in WhatsApp. The agent reads all three into one clean table.
2

Validate & normalize

OCR + rules turn every format into the same structure: worker, client, date, hours, rate, tips, deductions — flagging anything odd (missing rate, a 90-hour day, a name it doesn't recognize).

e.g. "J. Garcia — 8.0h Mon, 8.0h Tue… ✓ · 'M. Silva — 18h Wed' ⚠ flagged: unusually long shift, confirm?"
3

Reconcile — hours ⇄ pay (your #1 concern)

Before anything moves, the agent matches each worker's validated hours to what the check/ACH will be, and surfaces every mismatch to you — never silently.

e.g. "37 workers reconciled clean · 2 need you: Garcia's hours don't match last week's rate; Silva has hours from two clients — combine?"
4

You approve

You see the clean batch and the short list of flags on your phone. One tap approves; flags wait for your call. Nothing pays without this.

5

Into QuickBooks + payouts go out

Approved hours become QuickBooks Desktop entries (no retyping), and payouts run on the rails you already use — ACH/direct deposit and PayPal/Venmo/CashApp — with the check memo built your way.

e.g. 198 ACH + 2 PayPal sent · QB updated · digital receipts texted to each worker in their language.
6

Collections + the brief

Client invoices go out on their NET terms; late ones get chased politely and automatically. Monday morning you get a one-screen brief: who owes what, payroll ahead, margin per client.
