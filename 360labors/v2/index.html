The first thing we build — your #1 priority

Paying the workers, with accuracy and trust built in. This is the proof of concept.

The "hours → check" reconciliation engine. The AI reads the hours as they arrive — Excel, email, text, WhatsApp photos — into one clean, validated sheet per client per worker. Before a cent moves, it matches each worker's hours to the payment and flags any mismatch, gap, or duplicate for you — never silently. Then it prepares the run for QuickBooks Desktop and the payouts (ACH + the P2P apps you already use), with the check memo built exactly your way: hour count, range, tips, deductions.
