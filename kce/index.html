02 · The engine

What the Superclawman outreach engine does

This isn't a mail-merge tool. It's an autonomous SDR that runs on our agent infrastructure and plugs into yours. Already running live campaigns today — here's the capability set, in plain terms.

Prospecting

Finds and qualifies your buyers

  • Builds target lists by industry, geography, role (e.g. importers/exporters shipping into Miami, e-commerce brands needing 3PL)
  • Enriches each lead: verified email, phone, company context, recent signals
  • Scores fit before anyone gets contacted — no spray and pray
Personalization

Writes like a person who did research

  • Every first touch references the prospect's actual business — their lanes, products, pain
  • Speaks KCE's language and offer, in English, Spanish, or Portuguese per prospect
  • Per-contact memory: it remembers every prior exchange before it writes the next line
Persistence

Follows up so your team doesn't have to

  • Multi-touch sequences across channels, spaced and capped to stay welcome
  • Reply triage: interested → handoff; question → answered; objection → handled; not now → scheduled for later
  • Stops instantly on opt-out — suppression is permanent and automatic
Human control

Approval before anything sends

  • Draft cards go to your phone (or Slack) — approve, edit, or veto in one tap
  • Coach it in plain language ("be less formal", "always mention the portal") — it learns the directive permanently
  • Autonomy is a dial, not a switch: start fully supervised, loosen as trust builds
Visibility

You see everything

  • Every conversation logged and synced to your CRM (or a simple shared sheet if you don't run one)
  • Weekly digest: touches, replies, meetings booked, pipeline created
  • Slack alerts the moment a hot reply lands, with full thread context
Learning loop

Gets better every week

  • Tracks which messages, channels, and angles convert
  • Feeds wins back into the playbook automatically
  • Your team's edits become training signal — the engine converges on KCE's voice
03 · Channels

Four channels, one conversation

You mentioned WhatsApp, iMessage, Slack, and email. Here's the role each one plays — and the engine keeps a single memory per contact across all of them, so a prospect who answers an email on WhatsApp never gets asked the same question twice.

WhatsApp

The workhorse for Americas trade. Importers, exporters, and LatAm shippers live here. First touches, follow-ups, and full back-and-forth conversations — this is where our engine is most battle-tested.

iMessage

For US-based contacts where a blue bubble reads as a peer, not a vendor. Lighter volume, higher intimacy — great for warm follow-ups and referral-based intros.

Email

The formal channel: first contact for colder segments, proposals, recap documents. Sent from a properly warmed KCE sales identity with correct SPF/DKIM so deliverability stays clean.

Slack

KCE-internal: the engine's control room. Hot-reply alerts, approval cards, daily digests, and "@sdr what happened with prospect X?" on demand — inside the workspace your team already uses.

04 · How a campaign runs

From lead list to booked call

Safety is structural, not aspirational: rate caps per day and per contact, business-hours sending windows per timezone, no group blasts, permanent opt-out suppression, and an audit log of every message. The autonomy dial starts at "approve everything" and only moves when you move it.
