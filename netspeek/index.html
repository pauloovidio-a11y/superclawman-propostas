The proposition

Netspeek's Latin America conversations have stalled for one reason: there is no one in the region to carry them. I am proposing to be that person, under the Netspeek brand, with every transaction routed through a local partner so Netspeek carries no channel support burden and no currency or collections exposure.

No salary. No headcount line. No engineering prerequisite. Compensation deferred until revenue is collected.

Year-one cash exposure to Netspeek: approximately US$18,000 in travel and expenses. Nothing else.
01 / The problem this solves

A coverage gap, not a demand gap

Demand in the region is already reaching you. It has nowhere to land.

On our call you named three constraints with precision. Your first priority is a fast path to revenue. You cannot switch on many channels because you have no channel managers to support them. And you have had a handful of Mexico and Brazil approaches that went nowhere, in your words, because they were premature and nobody brought you anything.

Lotus ICT came to you. Globo is in conversation. Petrobras is in conversation. Every one of those threads needs someone in-region, in-language, who can qualify it, demo it, and close it without consuming your engineering team. That role normally costs a salary, a quota, and a year of ramp. This proposal removes almost all of that cost.

Why this is worth reading past page one

The question in front of you is not whether Latin America is worth entering. It is whether it can be entered without spending money you have not raised yet, and without taking Spencer's time away from North America. That is the question this document answers.

Why this is a now decision, not a next-year decision

Your own read is that the incumbents cannot follow quickly, because they are bolting AI onto platforms carrying years of technical debt while Lena was agentic from the first line. That advantage is real and it is temporary. Latin America is the one large region where none of them has moved yet and where, in your words, nothing is staked out.

There is also a calendar. The proactive troubleshooting agent reaches availability in October, and the seed round is in front of you. A region already opened, with named partners and a first reference customer, is a materially better story to raise on than a region described as a future opportunity. Starting now costs one quarter of travel. Starting after the raise means starting from zero with a number already committed to investors.

The economics the partner sells to itself

Brazilian integrators bill large enterprise accounts on the order of R$20,000 per month for room support, staffed with eight to ten technicians doing routine checks and truck rolls. Lena moves that routine tier to automation, so the same contract runs with one or two people working the genuinely hard problems.

The partner keeps the contract, improves its margin, protects itself against a competitor arriving with automation first, and adds recurring software revenue at 20 percent. I do not have to convince a partner of that. I have to show them the room-check demo once.

08 / Risks

What is not solved, stated plainly

You will take this document to people whose job is to find the hole in it. Here are the four I would raise first.

Product The interface is English only

Lena already responds in Portuguese, which I saw on our call. The console does not. This is a smaller obstacle than it looks: enterprise network and AV administrators in Brazil and Mexico already work in English-language management consoles every day. It is real for a wider mid-market push, and it gets funded by exactly the revenue this plan produces. Customers paying for the product is what pays for the engineers who translate it.

Product Yealink is not supported

Yealink's installed base in Latin America is significant and this will come up in the first ten conversations. I would rather sequence around it, leading with the accounts and partners where Poly, Cisco, Neat, Crestron and Zoom dominate, and bring you demand evidence to justify the integration rather than asking for it on faith.

Compliance SOC 2 is in process, and Brazil adds LGPD

Itaú and BTG will ask for both before a pilot, so financial services should not be the opening move. Media, energy, industrial and telecom will move first on a lightweight on-premise proxy. That sequencing costs nothing and avoids the longest security review in the region.

Execution I am unproven inside Netspeek

You have known me for years, but you have never seen me carry your number. That is precisely why the commitments in section five are small, dated and easy to check, and why the cost of finding out is roughly US$18,000. If I miss day 90, you have lost one quarter of travel budget and learned something real about the region at a very low price.

You told me your priority is a fast path to revenue, and that anything which pulls your team's attention away from North America has to justify itself. This is built to do exactly that: a region opened with two contracts instead of twenty, an entry cost of one quarter's travel, and a person who has already sold this category to these accounts through these partners.
